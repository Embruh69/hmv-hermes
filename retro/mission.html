THE SHIP

Meet the MSV Hermes — proud flagship of an eleven-vessel fleet, and proof that PRS builds to last. A licensed crew stands watch in careful rotation, and A.D.A.M. 7000, our shipboard intelligence, keeps every colonist safe, comfortable, and on schedule for arrival.

THE OPPORTUNITY

Proxima is waiting — a living world, roughly twenty years out at the Hermes' cruising speed. PRS is opening a limited number of colonist placements to new arrivals: quarters aboard the ship, a placement built around what you already know, and a real stake in the life waiting for you when you land.

LIFE ABOARD THE HERMES

Most colonists spend the crossing resting in cryo-sleep aboard the Chrono-Bay Wings, watched over by A.D.A.M. Once you're up, the Common Concourse is where the ship comes alive — a mess hall, a ration store, and colonists from every placement crossing paths like neighbors.
