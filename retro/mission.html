THE SHIP

Meet the MSV Hermes — flagship of the fleet, and proof that PRS builds to last. A licensed crew stands ready, and A.D.A.M. 7000, our shipboard intelligence, keeps every colonist safe, comfortable, and on schedule for arrival. Twenty years of trusted passage. Thousands of new beginnings.

THE OPPORTUNITY

Proxima is waiting — roughly twenty years out at the Hermes' cruising speed. PRS is opening colonist placements to anyone ready for a second beginning: quarters aboard the ship, a placement built around what you already know, and a real stake in the life waiting for you when you land. No spacefaring background required.

LIFE ABOARD THE HERMES

The easiest voyage you'll ever take. Spend it resting in cryo-sleep, watched over by A.D.A.M., until it's time to wake. No long years to endure, no dead time to dread — just a night's rest between you and a new life.
