OPEN ENROLLMENT

YOU DON'T NEED TO BE A SPACEFARER. YOU JUST NEED TO SAY YES.

WHO WE'RE LOOKING FOR

You. Whoever you are, whatever you've done. Former security contractors and corrections officers who couldn't find work groundside. Doctors, teachers, engineers, and analysts whose expertise deserved better than Earth ever gave it. Dockworkers, cargo handlers, and maintenance crews who've always worked with their hands. Every skill has a place on the Hermes, and every placement is a second chance. If any of that sounds like you — or like the person you used to be before life got in the way — PRS wants you on the manifest.

WHAT THE ROLE INVOLVES

Getting started is simple. Every applicant completes a Manifest Entry — your name, your background, your skills — the first page of your new colonist record. Beyond that, your story is yours to tell. We match placements to what you already bring, train you for everything else, and pair you with a case handler who's invested in seeing you settle in and thrive.

REQUIREMENTS

Ready to commit? Here's what it takes: you'll need to be 21 standard years or older to sign on independently, and prepared to see your full placement term through — this is a chance worth committing to, not a trial run. No prior spacefaring or technical experience needed; we'll handle that. And like any voyage into the unknown, this one carries real risk we won't pretend isn't there — by applying, you're telling us you understand that, and you're ready anyway.
