OPEN ENROLLMENT

PRS ISN'T LOOKING FOR SPACEFARERS

WHO WE'RE LOOKING FOR

Ordinary people, doing what they've always done. Former security contractors and corrections officers who couldn't find work groundside. Doctors, teachers, engineers, and analysts whose expertise didn't matter enough on Earth to keep them off the manifest. Dockworkers, cargo handlers, and maintenance crews who worked with their hands for a living. If that sounds like you — or like someone you used to be — PRS wants to hear from you.

WHAT THE ROLE INVOLVES

Every applicant completes a Manifest Entry — your name, your background, your skills — filed as part of the public colonist record. What you choose to share beyond that is entirely up to you. Placements are matched to your background wherever possible, with training provided for anything new, and a case handler to help you settle in.

REQUIREMENTS

Applicants must be 21 standard years or older to enter a PRS colonial placement contract independently. Placement aboard a vessel of this class carries inherent risk; PRS cannot guarantee applicant well-being is unaffected by conditions encountered during voyage or colonization, and by applying you acknowledge and accept this risk. This enrollment cycle covers a fixed placement term — applicants unable to commit for the full term should not apply. No prior spacefaring or technical experience is required.
