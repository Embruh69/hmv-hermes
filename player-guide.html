OPEN ENROLLMENT

YOU DON'T NEED TO BE A SPACEFARER. YOU JUST NEED TO SAY YES — TODAY.

WHO WE'RE LOOKING FOR

You. Whoever you are, whatever you've done. Former security contractors and corrections officers who couldn't catch a break groundside. Doctors, teachers, engineers, and analysts whose expertise deserved so much better than Earth ever gave it. Dockworkers, cargo handlers, and maintenance crews who've always worked with their hands and never got the credit. Every skill has a place on the Hermes, and every placement is a real second chance — not a consolation prize. If any of that sounds like you, or like the person you used to be before life got in the way, PRS wants you on the manifest. We mean that.

WHAT THE ROLE INVOLVES

Getting started is simple — genuinely, refreshingly simple. Every applicant completes a Manifest Entry: your name, your background, your skills. That's page one of your new colonist record. Beyond that, your story is yours to tell. We match your placement to what you already bring, train you for everything else, and pair you with a case handler who's personally invested in seeing you land on your feet and thrive.

REQUIREMENTS

Ready to commit? Here's exactly what it takes: you'll need to be 21 standard years or older to sign on independently, and prepared to see your full placement term through — this is a chance worth committing to, not a trial run you can walk back from. No prior spacefaring or technical experience needed; that part's on us. And like any voyage into the unknown, this one carries real risk we're not going to pretend away — by applying, you're telling us you understand that, and you're ready anyway. That's exactly the kind of colonist Proxima needs.
