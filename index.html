WHAT NEW COLONISTS ASK MOST

Do I need spacefaring experience?

No. PRS placements are matched to your background wherever possible, with training provided for anything new.

Is this placement permanent?

Yes. Colonist placements are one-way. Applicants should be able to commit to permanent relocation before applying.

What happens during the voyage?

Most colonists spend the crossing in cryo-sleep aboard the Chrono-Bay Wings, under A.D.A.M.'s care, until it's time to wake.

Can I bring family with me?

Every applicant is reviewed individually. Speak with a placement handler about your circumstances during your interview.

How do I begin my application?

Complete a Manifest Entry below. A PRS case handler will follow up to schedule your placement interview.
