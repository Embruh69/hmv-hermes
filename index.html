Do I need spacefaring experience?

Not even a little. Whatever you bring to the table, we'll build a placement around it — and train you for everything else. Nobody gets left behind for lack of experience. That's a promise, not a slogan.

Is this placement permanent?

Yes — and that's exactly the point. This isn't a vacation, it's a fresh start that sticks. Make sure you're ready to leave Earth behind for good, because once you're aboard, there's no looking back.

What happens during the voyage?

You rest. Most colonists spend the crossing in cryo-sleep aboard the Chrono-Bay Wings, watched over by A.D.A.M. the whole way — close your eyes on Earth, open them twenty years closer to Proxima. It really is that easy.

Can I bring family with me?

We know a decision like this never happens in isolation. Every application is reviewed individually, so bring your circumstances to a placement handler during your interview — we'll work it out together.

How do I begin my application?

One form. That's genuinely all it takes. Complete the application below and a PRS case handler will follow up fast to schedule your placement interview. Your new life starts with a single click.
