FAQS ABOUT PASSENGER

EVERYTHING BETWEEN YOU AND YOUR NEW LIFE

Do I need spacefaring experience?

Not even a little. Whatever you bring, we'll match a placement to it — and train you for the rest. Nobody gets left behind for lack of experience.

Is this placement permanent?

Yes — and that's the whole point. This isn't a visit, it's a fresh start. Make sure you're ready to leave Earth behind for good before you apply.

What happens during the voyage?

You rest. Most colonists spend the crossing in cryo-sleep aboard the Chrono-Bay Wings, watched over by A.D.A.M. the whole way — you'll close your eyes on Earth and open them twenty years closer to Proxima.

Can I bring family with me?

We know this decision doesn't happen in isolation. Every application is reviewed individually, so talk to a placement handler about your circumstances during your interview.

How do I begin my application?

One form is all it takes. Complete the application below and a PRS case handler will follow up to schedule your placement interview.
