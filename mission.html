OUR MISSION

TWENTY YEARS IN. RIGHT ON COURSE.

THE SHIP

Meet the MSV Hermes — proud flagship of an eleven-vessel fleet, and proof that PRS builds to last. Every hull in this fleet is voyage-tested and re-certified to the highest standard we can put our name behind. Onboard, you're never alone: a licensed crew stands watch in careful rotation, and A.D.A.M. 7000, our shipboard intelligence, keeps every colonist safe, comfortable, and on schedule for arrival. This is what twenty years of trusted passage looks like.

THE OPPORTUNITY

Proxima is waiting — a living world, roughly twenty years out at the Hermes' cruising speed, and the reason this entire program exists. PRS is opening a limited number of colonist placements to new arrivals, and each one comes with everything you need to start over: quarters aboard the ship, a placement built around what you already know, and a real stake in the life that's waiting for you when you land. No spacefaring background required — just the will to go.

LIFE ABOARD THE HERMES

The crossing is easier than you think. Most colonists spend it resting in cryo-sleep aboard the Chrono-Bay Wings, watched over by A.D.A.M. until it's time to wake — no long years to endure, just a night's rest away from a new life. Once you're up, the Common Concourse is where the ship comes alive: a mess hall, a ration store, community boards, and colonists from every placement crossing paths like neighbors. And if you ever need it, the Medical Bay is on call around the clock, A.D.A.M.-run and ready to walk you through anything, day or night.
