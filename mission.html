OUR MISSION

TWENTY YEARS INTO THE VOYAGE

THE SHIP

The MSV Hermes is one of eleven vessels Proxima Resettlement Solution committed to the resettlement effort — an older hull, patched and re-certified more times than her manifest cares to advertise, but voyage-proven all the same. She carries a limited cryo-sleep colonist complement, a licensed skeleton crew rotating in and out of stasis, and A.D.A.M. 7000, the shipboard intelligence responsible for keeping everyone alive, asleep, and on schedule for the crossing.

THE OPPORTUNITY

Proxima is a marginally habitable world roughly twenty years out at the Hermes' cruising speed — everything this program exists to reach. PRS is opening a limited number of colonist placements to new arrivals. You won't be trained crew. You'll be given quarters, a placement suited to your background, and a stake in the life waiting on arrival.

LIFE ABOARD THE HERMES

Most colonists spend the crossing resting in cryo-sleep aboard the Chrono-Bay Wings, under A.D.A.M.'s care, until it's time to wake. Once up, the Common Concourse is the ship's social hub — mess hall, ration store, community boards — the one place aboard where colonists from every placement naturally cross paths. The Medical Bay is automated and A.D.A.M.-run, built for routine care, with a steady voice to walk you through anything minor.
