OUR MISSION

TWENTY YEARS OF NEW BEGINNINGS. AND COUNTING.

THE SHIP

Meet the MSV Hermes — flagship of an eleven-vessel fleet and living proof that PRS builds to last. Every hull in this fleet is voyage-tested, re-certified, and held to the highest standard we're willing to put our name on. Onboard, you are never alone: a licensed crew stands watch in careful rotation, and A.D.A.M. 7000, our shipboard intelligence, keeps every colonist safe, comfortable, and on schedule for arrival. Twenty years of trusted passage and thousands of new beginnings. That's the Hermes.

THE OPPORTUNITY

Proxima is waiting — a living world, roughly twenty years out at the Hermes' cruising speed, and the entire reason this program exists. PRS is opening new colonist placements to anyone ready for a second beginning, and every one comes fully loaded: quarters aboard the ship, a placement built around what you already know, and a real, legal stake in the life waiting for you when you land. No spacefaring background required. No résumé gatekeeping. Just the will to go — the rest is on us.

LIFE ABOARD THE HERMES

The crossing is easier than you think — genuinely. Most colonists spend it resting in cryo-sleep aboard the Chrono-Bay Wings, watched over by A.D.A.M. until it's time to wake. No long years to endure, no dead time to dread — just a night's rest between you and a new life. Once you're up, the Common Concourse is where the ship comes alive: a mess hall, a ration store, community boards, and colonists from every placement crossing paths like neighbors. And if you ever need it, the Medical Bay is staffed around the clock, A.D.A.M.-run and ready to walk you through anything, day or night. You are never on your own out here.
