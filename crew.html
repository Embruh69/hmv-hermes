OUR CREW

THE CREW BEHIND YOUR VOYAGE

Four people stand watch over the rest of the Hermes' voyage — and over every colonist who comes aboard. Meet the crew getting you to Proxima.

Ser Ed Sheeran

Captain, MSV Hermes

Before Hermes, Ed Sheeran spent 10 years as a captain and 5 years as a co-captain, primarily on Boeing-type. Building a reputation for a great pilot with minimum of failure, and always found a way out at situation under pressure. At his thirty fresh out of the program 5 years before deployment beyond the earth, he was knighted by the Queen. Recognition not for years of service alone, but for being the one chosen to go first.

Louis Tomlinson

Quartermaster

Louis came up through logistics and freight work, not a command track. The kind of job where a miscount means someone doesn't eat. That instinct carried straight into his selection for Hermes. When the program needed a Quartermaster who could keep a scarce, closed system balanced for years without slipping, left Louis the only choice for the position.
